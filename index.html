Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you understand Psoria-AI.

What is Psoria-AI?

Psoria-AI is a platform designed to help people track their psoriasis, understand possible patterns, and have clearer conversations with their dermatologist.

Can Psoria-AI diagnose psoriasis?

No. Psoria-AI is designed to help you track symptoms, understand patterns, and prepare clearer information for your dermatologist. It does not replace a medical diagnosis or professional medical advice.

How does Psoria-AI find possible triggers?

Psoria-AI looks at the information you record over time, such as your symptoms, sleep, mood, weather, and daily habits. It looks for patterns that may be linked to your flares and helps you understand what might be affecting your symptoms.

Can I share my information with my dermatologist?

Yes. Psoria-AI is designed to help you organize your symptoms, photos, progress, and insights into clear information that you can share with your dermatologist during your appointments.

Is my health information private?

Psoria-AI is being designed with privacy in mind. Your health information should be handled securely and only used to provide the features you choose to use. We will clearly explain how your data is collected, stored, and used.

Do I need to use Psoria-AI every day?

Daily tracking can help Psoria-AI find useful patterns over time, but you don't need to be perfect. The goal is to make tracking simple enough to fit into your routine and help you understand your psoriasis over time.

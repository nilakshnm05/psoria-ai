Everything you need to manage psoriasis — in one place

Track symptoms, uncover personal triggers with AI, monitor what treatments actually work, and share clear reports with your dermatologist. No more scattered notes, guesswork, or feeling lost between appointments — just a simple, science-backed system that helps you feel more in control of your skin, every day.

📋

Understand Your Symptoms

Track your symptoms in seconds. Rate your itch, redness, pain, and scaling on a simple scale. Mark affected areas on a body map and add notes about your mood, sleep, and weather.

💡

Discover Your Triggers

Finally see what may be behind your flares. PsoriaAI looks at your daily logs to find patterns, such as stress or poor sleep being linked with worse symptoms.

📸

See Your Progress

Track your progress with photos, not guesses. Capture photos, get AI-assisted notes about how your skin looks, and share them with your dermatologist. Visual tracking and simple severity scores help you and your doctor see how your skin changes over time.

🧠

Feel More In Control

Turn your daily information into something you can understand. See your symptoms, triggers, photos, and progress together so you can make better-informed decisions and have more useful conversations with your dermatologist.
